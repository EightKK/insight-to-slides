# Analytical Reasoning and Counterhypotheses

## Decompose before interpreting
Separate revenue growth from volume, mix, unit pricing, currency translation, scope changes and financial-services revenue. Separate operating from financial / other income and the net profit bridge. Never map a numerator from one scope onto a denominator from another without a normalization discussion.

## Counterhypothesis matrix
For each hypothesis record: expected observation, supportive evidence IDs, contradictory evidence IDs, alternate explanation and a falsifier. Give a calibrated confidence (`high`,`medium`,`low`,`undetermined`) and *never* treat 'not disproven' as proven.

## Materiality
Prioritize whether an observation changes one of: (i) primary conclusion, (ii) management decision, (iii) key risk, (iv) next-data request. Avoid endless broad contextual scraping. When data are undisclosed, do not backsolve a precise estimate from ambiguous residuals.

## Company financial research: special traps
- Automotive OEM consolidated sales may include financial services; 'average revenue per vehicle' is often invalid.
- Deconsolidation can move historical vehicle counts and financial statement components. Flag both as published and comparable scope.
- Share sale and FX gains may drive pre-tax / attributable income without reflecting production economics.
- Margin change is a percentage-point calculation, not relative percentage growth.
- Peer consolidated profits can be driven by motorcycles/financial services and must be labeled accordingly.
- Outlook bridge is a revision to a forecast, not an observed year-on-year movement.
- FX effects based on management sensitivity are not automatically equal to constant-currency earnings.
- Positive published currency contributions do not make every remaining negative factor a pure operational loss; some adjustments (e.g. unrealised consolidated profit eliminations) are themselves FX-related.
- A product line can gain volume and share while total volume falls; do not infer product-level profitability that is not disclosed.
- A peer's different result is counter-evidence to a company-specific explanation only when scope is comparable; label non-like-for-like peers.
- Fiscal-year labels differ between companies (some use the starting year, some the ending year). Always state the actual date range.
- Check the as-of date: a results release scheduled after it does not exist yet. Never describe a forecast as an actual.
- When a source declines to disclose a figure, record a limitation; never back-solve a precise number.

## Required outputs
- Decision question; ranked data gaps; evidence ledger; completed and unanswered hypotheses; verified numeric derivations; communication brief; Japanese conclusions with explicit unknowns.

## Material claim challenge loop

For the most important 3-5 claims, record the exact dataset/scope, competing mechanism, best available counter-evidence, and what observation would reverse the current view. Recompute group/segment reconciliation with central elimination rather than forcing segment changes to total. If revenue grows 29% and adjusted SG&A expense rises 10%, falling SG&A-to-revenue ratio does NOT mean absolute cost cutting. Explicitly annotate management's estimates and identify separate price, volume, mix and timing effects when disclosed; do not convert one into the other.
